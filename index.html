Discover My Story

From Family Dealership to AI & Cloud Innovation

My engineering intuition didn't begin inside a textbook—it was forged in Junnar, Maharashtra, observing the moving gears, complex diagnostics, and operational logistics of our family vehicle dealership. Immersed in mechanical assemblies and customer troubleshooting from a young age, I developed a deep appreciation for how interconnected parts work together under stress. That foundational curiosity soon migrated into computing: translating physical engines into software algorithms, machine intelligence, and distributed architectures.

Today, as a third-year AI & Data Science undergraduate at Anantrao Pawar College of Engineering and Research (APCOE&R), Pune, affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University (graduating in 2028), I channel that mindset into engineering resilient AI systems and leading vibrant developer communities. I am honored to serve as an AWS Student Builder Campus Leader 2026 and AWS Student Builder Group Co-Organizer, while driving campus technical education as a GeeksforGeeks Campus Mantri and collaborating as a Microsoft Learn and Google Student Ambassador. My goal is simple: build high-utility intelligent software and mentor the next wave of engineers to deploy with confidence.

“Great engineering is the art of turning raw complexity into reliable, accessible tools for everyday people.”

"Saad. Start kardo." — Between University Exams & Zero Funding

My university exams were going on. Between my last two papers — Environmental Studies and Project Management — every logical voice in my head said:

"Saad, study kar. Saad, exams pehle. Saad, baad mein kar."
But my heart said just three words: "Saad. Start kardo." 🇮🇳

Today I walked into a school that has stood for 100 years — Mahatma Jyotirao Phule High School, Rasta Peth, Pune — built on the belief that every child deserves education, regardless of where they come from.

100 Students in One Room
Day 1 Zero Funding Start
100 Yrs School Heritage

Students came from every corner of Maharashtra, ranging from Nagpur to Nandurbar and Jalgaon. Some had never seen AI in their lives. Then, a student whispered:

"AI ne amchi shabd aikla?" ("Did AI... listen to my word?") 🎵

In that moment, I understood something no university exam could teach me. The courage to start between two exam papers with zero funding and one mission: giving young minds the skills, confidence, and belief that their voice matters.

Transforming Young Minds with AI – Day 02 🤖✨

I had the opportunity to conduct the second day of my AI learning workshop at Mahatma Jyotirao Phule High School, Nana Peth, Pune, where I introduced students from Classes 5th to 10th to the fascinating world of Artificial Intelligence.

The curiosity, excitement, and creativity shown by these young learners were truly inspiring. It was amazing to see students exploring how AI can be used not only for technology but also for storytelling, creativity, and learning.

🧠 Transformer Foundations:

We explored the journey of Transformers in AI — from early developments to the revolutionary 2017 research paper “Attention Is All You Need”, which introduced the Transformer architecture and revolutionized modern AI.

Students moved from concepts to creativity by using AI tools to:
  • 🎨 Comic Books: Create cartoon-style comic strips
  • 📖 Visual Stories: Turn imaginative ideas into visual narratives
  • 🎬 AI Movies: Generate short AI-powered movies from historical topics
🚩
Historic Workshop Highlight:

Creating a 10-second AI movie based on the history of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj 🚩. Watching students’ reactions as history came alive through AI-generated visuals was an unforgettable moment!

This experience reminded me that when young minds get access to the right tools and guidance, their imagination has no limits. A small step towards building an AI-ready generation. 🌱
